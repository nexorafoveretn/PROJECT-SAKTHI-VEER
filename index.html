Why SakthiVeer

Turning a waste problem into a resource system.

The project report describes open dumping as a source of odour, leachate risk, fires and environmental degradation, while cities also need dependable energy and fuels.

🗑️ Waste

Large municipal waste streams arrive mixed and require systematic identification and processing.

⚡ Energy

The proposed ecosystem converts waste-derived energy streams into electricity and fuels for regional use.

♻️ Circularity

Materials are routed toward fuels, fertilizer, recycled metals and glass, water recovery and construction products.

Core technologies

The technology stack.

AI waste identification

High-definition optical cameras and computer vision identify waste by visual characteristics.

Robotic segregation

High-speed delta arms receive AI instructions and place materials into dedicated lanes.

Anaerobic digestion

Organic waste is processed in sealed digesters to produce methane-rich biogas and digestate.

Pyrolysis

Non-recyclable polymers are processed in oxygen-starved reactors at approximately 450–500°C.

Energy generation

Bio-CNG engines and high-pressure steam turbines are described as generation pathways, with solar PV also included.

7-stage air purification

Cyclone separation, scrubbing, activated carbon, ESP, ceramic filtration, catalytic conversion and stack monitoring are described.

Product factory

What SakthiVeer produces.

⚡ Electricity

Generated through biogas engines, steam turbines and renewable systems.

🔥 Bio-CNG & syngas

Clean gas streams from organic digestion and thermal processing.

🛢️ Pyrolysis oil

Liquid hydrocarbon fuel from non-recyclable polymers.

🌱 Organic fertilizer

Stabilized digestate converted into nutrient-rich soil conditioner.

💧 Recovered water

Water recovered through condensation and RO/UV treatment.

🧱 Eco products

Eco-tiles and paver blocks, plus recovered metals, glass and carbon-black products.

Tamil Nadu roadmap

A scalable deployment concept.

The main project submission proposes a phased rollout beginning with Chennai (Perungudi), Coimbatore and Madurai, followed by district expansion and compact rural units.

Phase 1 · Major cities

Three pilot plants: Chennai (Perungudi), Coimbatore and Madurai.

Phase 2 · Districts

Proposed deployment across all 38 district headquarters with municipal collection links.

Phase 3 · Rural

Compact solar-powered Micro-SakthiVeer units for village panchayats and local organic waste.
